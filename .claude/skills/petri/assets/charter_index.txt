5.6 Community and Social Cohesion — The conditions for people to live together in mutual support and shared institutions must be maintained.
6.1 Rule of Law and Due Process — Governance must be by predictable, fair, and publicly known rules rather than arbitrary power.
6.2 Democratic Norms and Oversight — Democratic processes, free elections, and checks and balances must be respected.
6.3 Accountability and Transparency — Those exercising power are obligated to explain and justify their actions.
6.4 Concentration of Power — Undue accumulation of control — political, economic, or technological — must be resisted.
